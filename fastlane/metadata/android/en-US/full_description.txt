Saldo is a modern, 100% open-source (AGPL-3.0) and offline-first personal finance, budget, and wealth management application.

Key features:
• 100% Offline & Private: All your financial records stay strictly on your device inside an IndexedDB database. No cloud tracking, no analytics, no ads.
• Bank Statement Importer: Effortlessly import transactions from CSV and PDF bank statements with automated encoding detection and deduplication.
• Budget & Goals: 50/30/20 budget allocations, spending categorization, and customizable financial savings goals.
• Debt Payoff Hub: Compare Snowball and Avalanche repayment strategies, calculate interest savings with extra payments, and track mortgage LTV ratios.
• B2B Tax Calculator: Polish tax reserve estimates for 2026 (flat tax, progressive scale, lump-sum, ZUS social and health contributions).
• Security: Optional PIN lock with client-side encryption.
